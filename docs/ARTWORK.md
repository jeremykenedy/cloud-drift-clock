# Artwork and footage

## Sunset cloud flight

The scene uses an excerpt of [breathtaking sunset and cloudsurfing lake Lucerne (Vierwaldstaettersee) 4k cinematic DJI fpv oneshot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kKPvF0rwqHA), published February 4, 2022 by [anotherview](https://www.youtube.com/@anotherview1). The primary upload explicitly marks the video Creative Commons Attribution, reuse allowed. It is credited under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The actual downloaded source is 3840 by 2160 at 25fps. The app bundles 1920 by 1080 and 1280 by 720 H264 derivatives. That does not establish native 4K output. Source 213.5-248.7 seconds is edited with a 1.2-second dissolve, producing a 34-second loop. The start follows the first 1.2 seconds so the end returns to adjacent source motion. The scene is an edited loop, not a naturally closed camera path.

Source audio is omitted, including the separately credited music. There is no digital image pan, synthetic cloud layer, artificial density control or lighting grade. The actual flight includes banking and distant mountain scenery. Required attribution and the complete license are bundled in the offline Credits screen, outside the word-free film.
